# 📌 Personally-Identifiable-Information (PII)

**Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** refers to any data that can be used to **identify, contact, or locate** an individual — either directly or indirectly.

---

## 🔍 Common Examples of PII

| Type        | Examples                                                                 |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Direct PII**   | Full name, Social Security Number (SSN), Passport number, etc.         |
| **Indirect PII** | Date of birth, gender, ZIP code, IP address, job title, etc.           |
| **Sensitive PII**| Biometric data, health records, financial info, login credentials      |

---

## 🔒 Sensitive vs Non-Sensitive PII

| Category        | Description                                                                 |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Sensitive PII**   | Requires extra protection due to potential for harm if disclosed (e.g., SSN, bank info) |
| **Non-Sensitive PII** | Publicly available info (e.g., name, workplace) unless combined with other data |

---

## 💥 Risks of PII Exposure
- Identity theft  
- Fraud and scams  
- Reputation damage  
- Legal and compliance violations (e.g., **GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA**)  

---
## 🔐 PII in Compliance & Regulations

### 📜 GDPR, HIPAA & CCPA – Overview  

---

## 🇪🇺 GDPR – General Data Protection Regulation  

- 🌍 **Region:** European Union (EU)  
- 📅 **Effective:** May 25, 2018  
- 🎯 **Focus:** Protects personal data & privacy of EU citizens.  
- 👤 **Scope:** Applies to any company (worldwide) that processes data of EU residents.  

### ✅ Key Principles:
- 🔒 Lawfulness, fairness, transparency  
- 🎯 Purpose limitation  
- 📉 Data minimization  
- ⏳ Storage limitation  
- 🔑 Integrity & confidentiality  

### ⚠️ Penalties:  
- Up to **€20 million** or **4% of global annual turnover** (whichever is higher).  

---

## 🇺🇸 HIPAA – Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act  

- 🌍 **Region:** United States (US)  
- 📅 **Effective:** 1996 (Privacy & Security Rules later updates)  
- 🎯 **Focus:** Protects **health information** (PHI – Protected Health Information).  
- 👤 **Scope:** Healthcare providers, insurers, business associates.  

### ✅ Key Rules:
- 🏥 **Privacy Rule** – protects patient information.  
- 🔐 **Security Rule** – ensures confidentiality, integrity, availability of ePHI.  
- 📑 **Breach Notification Rule** – requires notice of data breaches.  

### ⚠️ Penalties:  
- Civil penalties up to **$1.5M per year** for violations.  
- Criminal penalties including fines & imprisonment.  

---

## 🇺🇸 CCPA – California Consumer Privacy Act  

- 🌍 **Region:** California, USA  
- 📅 **Effective:** January 1, 2020  
- 🎯 **Focus:** Gives California residents rights over their **personal data**.  
- 👤 **Scope:** Businesses that collect/process personal data of California residents.  

### ✅ Key Rights:
- 👀 Right to know what personal data is collected.  
- 🗑️ Right to delete personal data.  
- 🚫 Right to opt-out of data sale.  
- ⚖️ Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.  

### ⚠️ Penalties:  
- $2,500 per violation (unintentional).  
- $7,500 per violation (intentional).  

---

## 🆚 Quick Comparison  

| Regulation | Region 🌍 | Focus 🎯 | Applies To 👤 | Penalties ⚠️ |
|------------|-----------|----------|---------------|--------------|
| **GDPR**  | EU 🇪🇺 | Personal data & privacy | Any company handling EU data | €20M or 4% of global turnover |
| **HIPAA** | US 🇺🇸 | Health data (PHI/ePHI) | Healthcare orgs & partners | $1.5M/year + criminal penalties |
| **CCPA**  | California 🇺🇸 | Consumer personal data rights | Businesses with CA data | $2,500–$7,500 per violation |

## ✅ PII Protection Best Practices

- 🔒 Use **encryption** for storage and transmission  
- 📉 Apply **data minimization** (only collect what's necessary)  
- 🗑️ Perform **regular data purging**  
- ⛔ Disable **auto-log of sensitive fields** (e.g., passwords, tokens)  
- 🟢 Implement **access control & auditing**
